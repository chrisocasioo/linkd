How it works

From download to first introduction in minutes

Build your card

Add your name, photo, and the links that matter: phone, email, website, socials, even Venmo.

Share it your way

Tap to send a link, show your QR code, or drop it in Apple Wallet. Your card lives at linkd.biz/you.

Keep every contact

When someone shares their details back, they land in your Linkd contacts, ready for your follow-up.

Features

Everything a business card should do, and a few things paper can't

Multiple cards

Keep a Work card, a Personal card, a side-hustle card. Each has its own photo, colors, font, and links.

Share any way you like

Send a link, show a QR code, AirDrop it, or add it to Apple Wallet. Anyone can open your card, no app needed.

Branded QR codes

Every card gets a QR code in black and gold with the Linkd mark. Pro lets you pick your own colors and logo.

Business card scanner

Snap a photo of a paper card and Linkd reads it on your device and files it in your contacts.

Built-in contacts

People who open your card can send their details back to you. Everyone you meet lives in one tidy address book.

See who is looking

Pro shows how often each card is viewed and which links get tapped, so you know what is working.

Make it yours

A QR code that looks like your brand

Every Linkd card comes with a striking black-and-gold QR code. Upgrade to Pro to match your own colors and put your logo in the center.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do the people I share with need the app?

No. Your card opens as a clean web page in any browser, with a button to save you straight to their contacts.

What does my card link look like?

Your cards live at linkd.biz/yourname/card-name, for example linkd.biz/alex/work. You choose your username when you sign up and can change it later in Settings. Old links keep redirecting.

Is Linkd free?

Yes. You can create up to five cards, share them, and scan business cards for free. Linkd Pro adds unlimited cards, analytics, and QR customization.

Who can see my card?

Anyone you send the link or QR code to. Only put on a card what you are happy for them to see.

How do I cancel Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Linkd.

Can I delete my data?

Yes, any time, from Settings in the app. Your account, cards, contacts, and photos are permanently deleted right away. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
